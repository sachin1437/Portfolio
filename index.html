A real-time AI threat-detection platform for hotels, schools and institutions. A YOLOv8 vision backbone detects fire, smoke, weapons and people from a live video feed, with a multi-layer suppression engine to cut false alarms. A deep-learning system that recognises Indian Sign Language gestures and converts them to text, for more accessible communication. It pairs MediaPipe hand and pose tracking with a BiLSTM sequence model across 19 gesture classes, and is written up as a research paper.
